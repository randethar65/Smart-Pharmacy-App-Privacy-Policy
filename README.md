# Privacy Policy — SmartPharmacy

**Last updated:** September 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how SmartPharmacy ("the App", "we", "us") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our mobile application.

## Who We Are

SmartPharmacy is an independently developed online pharmacy app that connects patients with a pharmacy for browsing, ordering, and prescription-based purchases.

**Developer contact:** randethar65@gmail.com

## Information We Collect

### Account information
When you register, we collect your full name, username, email address, password (stored securely, never in plain text), and phone number.

### Order & delivery information
When you place an order, we collect your delivery city, street address, phone number, and an approximate delivery location (map pin) to fulfill and deliver your order.

### Prescription images
If you order a prescription-only medicine, you upload a photo of your prescription. This image is stored securely and is only accessible to you and the reviewing pharmacist, for the purpose of verifying and approving your order.

### Profile picture
You may optionally upload a profile picture (avatar).

### Payment information
Payments are processed by **Stripe**, a third-party payment processor. SmartPharmacy does not collect, see, or store your card number, expiry date, or CVC — these are sent directly to Stripe. We only receive confirmation that a payment succeeded or failed.

### Notifications
We store in-app notifications related to your orders and prescriptions (e.g., order shipped, prescription approved/rejected) so you can view your order history and status.

## How We Use Your Information

We use the information above to:
- Create and manage your account
- Process and deliver your orders
- Verify and review prescription-only purchases
- Process payments (via Stripe)
- Send you order and prescription status notifications
- Maintain the security of your account and our systems

We do **not** sell your personal data to third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.

## Who We Share Data With

- **Stripe** — to process payments securely.
- We do not share your prescription images, medical information, or personal details with any other third party.

## Data Security

- Passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text.
- All communication between the app and our servers is encrypted (HTTPS).
- Prescription images are protected behind authorization checks — only you and the reviewing pharmacist can access them.
- Access to administrative and pharmacist features requires a verified account role; new accounts cannot self-assign these roles.

## Data Retention & Deletion

We retain your account and order data for as long as your account is active, or as needed to comply with legal obligations. If you would like your account and associated personal data deleted, contact us at **randethar65@gmail.com** and we will process your request.

## Children's Privacy

SmartPharmacy is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
